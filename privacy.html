STORY SPILL

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026. Story Spill is a personal application that runs locally on its owner's computer.

Information and Google data

When the owner connects YouTube, Story Spill requests authorization to upload videos, read details about the connected YouTube channel and its videos, and read that channel's YouTube Analytics. It uses this data only to publish owner-approved videos, display video status and performance, and help the owner's local AI improve future story ideas.

Storage and sharing

Story scripts, rendered videos, settings, analytics snapshots, and Google OAuth credentials are stored locally on the owner's computer. Story Spill does not sell personal information, share it with advertisers, or use Google user data for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains on the owner's computer until the owner deletes it. The owner can disconnect Story Spill from YouTube inside the application and can revoke access at any time from Google Account permissions. Revoking access prevents future access to Google user data. The owner can also delete locally stored credentials and analytics from the application data folder.
